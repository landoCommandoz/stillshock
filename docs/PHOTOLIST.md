# Photo list for Stillshock examples

All photos are Lando's own, so no likeness questions. Phone camera is fine. Shoot in
portrait (tall) because the app crops to 9:16 by default. Keep the subject in the middle
60 percent of the frame with room above the head and below the feet, because the crop
trims the sides and the effects need space to move. Daylight or a bright room. No logos,
no text, no other people in the background.

Eight photos cover all twelve effects. Shoot 2 or 3 variations of each.

| # | Photo | Effects it feeds | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You, full body, standing on a plain street or in a park, arms relaxed, looking at camera | Disintegrate, Levitate, Golden Statue, Zero-G Blast | A full silhouette is what these effects transform |
| 2 | You, head and shoulders, neutral face, plain wall behind | Liquid Metal, Glitch, Deep Freeze | Close faces sell the surface effects and the ice |
| 3 | You, mid-celebration pose (arms up, big smile), indoors | Confetti Burst | The model keeps the pose and adds the party |
| 4 | A pet (yours or a friend's) sitting and facing camera | Confetti, Levitate, Googly-style fun | Pets are the most shared subject |
| 5 | A product on a table: a sneaker, a watch, a coffee cup, a bottle | Liquid Metal, Golden Statue, Inferno, Deep Freeze | Products are safe, clean and look expensive after the effect |
| 6 | A wide landscape or skyline, no people: beach, field, rooftop view | Lightning Strike, Cloud Dive, Portal | Big sky gives the storm and the camera pull-back room |
| 7 | A car or a bike, three-quarter angle, open space around it | Inferno, Zero-G Blast, Portal | Hard objects react well to fire and shockwaves |
| 8 | A doorway, arch or hallway with depth behind it | Portal, Glitch | Depth makes the portal read as real |

Bonus for the demo: one strong photo of you (number 1 or 2) is the hero. The demo runs
five effects on that one photo to show "same photo, five effects."

Send them in a shared Google Drive folder called `stillshock-photos`, or upload to
`examples/raw/` in the GitHub app. JPEG or PNG, any size; the browser crops them.
